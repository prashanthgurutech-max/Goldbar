What Affects Your Loan Amount?

📊
LTV (Loan-to-Value) Ratio RBI mandates a maximum 75% LTV. So for gold worth ₹1 lakh, you can borrow up to ₹75,000. Banks are conservative; NBFCs offer closer to 75%.
⚖️
Assay Value (not market price) Lenders use their own assayed value, typically 5–15% below market price to cover risk. The loan is based on assay value × LTV, not the spot price.
⏱️
Tenure and repayment type Gold loans are typically 3–24 months. Options: bullet repayment (pay everything at end), EMI, or overdraft. Bullet suits short-term cash needs.
📋
Minimum gold accepted Most lenders require minimum 18K hallmarked gold. Coins accepted only up to 50g (per RBI guidelines). No stone-studded jewellery.
ℹ️ Loan amounts are estimates based on publicly available LTV ratios and interest rates (Oct 2026). Actual loan amount depends on lender's assay, your credit profile, and applicable charges. Always confirm with the lender before pledging gold.
