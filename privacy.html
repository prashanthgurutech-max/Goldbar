Privacy

GoldRadar does not ask you to sign in and does not ask for your name, email or phone number.

What we count

To see how many people use the site we keep a simple anonymous counter. It records:

  • a random id stored in your browser (not linked to your name or device);
  • the time of the visit and whether you used a phone, tablet or computer;
  • the website you came from (for example WhatsApp or Google), if your browser shares it;
  • which weight, karat and Buy buttons you used.

We do not store your IP address in this counter. If your browser's "Do Not Track" setting is on, nothing is counted. You can clear the random id any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.

If you choose a language or a city, the site remembers that choice in your browser so it opens the same way next time. It is not sent anywhere.

The Gold tracker saves the purchases you enter only in your own browser. They are not sent to us or to anyone else, and they are lost if you clear your browser data, so use the backup download.

Services involved

  • Hosting: the site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitor IP addresses under its own privacy policy.
  • Counter storage: the anonymous counter is stored with Supabase.
  • Fonts: your browser loads fonts from Google Fonts.
  • Store links: when you tap Buy you leave GoldRadar. The store (and any affiliate network) then handles your data under its own privacy policy and may set its own cookies.
