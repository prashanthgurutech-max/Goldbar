All BIS Hallmark Codes

BIS hallmarking is mandatory in India since June 2021. Look for the BIS logo (triangle), hallmark centre number, and purity code on your jewellery.

How to Verify at the Jeweller

🔎
Look for 4 hallmark stamps BIS logo (Bureau of Indian Standards triangle), 6-digit HUID number, purity in fineness (e.g. 916), and jeweller's identification mark.
📱
Use BIS Care App Scan the 6-digit HUID on the jewellery using the official BIS Care app to verify authenticity instantly. Available on Play Store and App Store.
⚖️
Ask for making charges separately Making charges should be quoted per gram or as a %. Typical range: ₹200–600/g for machine-made, ₹500–2000/g for handmade. Always negotiable.
🧮
Calculate fair price yourself Fair price = (Today's 24K rate × purity) × weight + making charges + 3% GST. Ask the jeweller to show the breakup in writing.
ℹ️ Purity percentages are based on BIS (Bureau of Indian Standards) hallmarking standards. Always verify jewellery at a BIS-licensed hallmarking centre for certified results.
